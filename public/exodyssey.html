⚓ Ex Odyssey the ship-maker — a 1,000-hull fleet for your table ← Tracker
Ex Odyssey is an unofficial, procedurally-generated ship-maker built as a companion to D&D Tracker Pro, in the spirit of table generators like Ex Novo & Ex Umbra.
Every hull is drawn from a fixed corpus of 1,000. Map prompts are written for image generators (paste into your tool of choice).
